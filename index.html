How it works

01

One thank-you each, once a month

That's the whole rule. No budget, no limit. If six people helped you, thank six; if eleven did, thank eleven. What keeps it honest isn't scarcity — it's rule 02.

02

A reason is required

No reason, no thank-you. One line about what the person actually did — and writing thirteen true ones is real work, which is exactly why nobody will thank everyone out of politeness.

03

You can't stack them

One thank-you per person per month, so nobody can pile several on a favourite. Next month the slate is clear and you can thank them again.

04

Seven months, September to March

A month each for Sep, Oct, Nov, Dec, Jan, Feb and Mar. Counts reset on the 1st; the running total doesn't.

05

Most thanks takes the month

The winner picks any one item from the shelf, and the company buys it.

06

Win once, and that's your month

A monthly winner is out of the monthly race for the rest of the season. Keep receiving thanks — they all still count toward the Champion — but if you top the board again, the prize passes to the highest scorer who hasn't won yet. Seven months, thirteen people: everyone has a real shot.

07

Highest total on 31 March is Champion

Every thank-you from all seven months, added up — including the months someone sat out. The Champion gets a ₹50,000 travel allowance. Tie-break: whoever was thanked in more separate months.

08

Aayush moderates, and can't win

Thank him if he helped you and it shows on the board like anyone else's. He's just not eligible for a monthly prize or the Champion — the person running the thing shouldn't be collecting from it.

09

The shelf changes

Nothing here is fixed. An item comes off if its price climbs mid-season, if the quality turns out not to match the photos, or if getting it to you is more trouble than it's worth — and new things get added all the way through. Anything on the Rewards tab can be removed at any time.

10

Claim it or lose it

A win isn't credit you can bank. No saving it for something better later, no rolling two months into one bigger gift. Pick from what's on the shelf the month you win, within two weeks of the month closing, or it passes.

11

Your name goes on it

Every thank-you is public and signed — the reason and who sent it. Nothing here is anonymous, which is also what keeps anyone from thanking people on someone else's behalf.

12

Changed your mind?

Take a thank-you back any time before the month closes and the bead goes with it. Nothing is locked until the 1st.

One honest caveat. Names are self-selected, not logged in — the board trusts you to pick your own. In a team of fourteen that's fine, and it keeps the thing frictionless.
